Academic Profile

About

Beyond Headlines. Understanding the World.

I write about how power, geography and history shape international politics, with a particular focus on Bangladesh and South Asia. This site brings together my analysis, research and sources.

My aim is to look beyond the headlines: the historical context, geopolitical interests, strategic calculations and political forces behind international events.

I try to explain complex developments clearly, show my evidence and sources, and keep facts separate from opinion.

  • IndependentA personal project with no advertising.
  • Source-basedFactual claims are cited and linked where possible.
  • Regional focusBangladesh and South Asia, in global context.

Mission

My mission is to make complex geopolitical and political developments more understandable through analytical writing, historical context and critical examination.

I aim to:

  1. Explain international affairs with historical and geographical context, beyond surface-level news.
  2. Examine Bangladesh’s politics, history, governance and foreign relations within the regional and global system.
  3. Keep facts and opinion apart, show my sources, and encourage critical, informed discussion.

Analytical Approach

Meaningful analysis requires more than following events. My approach emphasises:

01

Historical Context

Understanding how the past shapes the present.

02

Geographical Perspective

How geography, resources, borders and strategic locations influence political decisions.

03

Multiple Perspectives

Weighing competing interests, narratives and interpretations.

04

Critical Thinking

Questioning assumptions, examining evidence and separating facts from opinion.

05

Analytical Depth

Asking not only what is happening, but why, and what it may mean.

Editorial Standards

How I try to keep this work reliable.

01

Sources

I cite sources for factual claims and link to them where I can.

02

Facts and opinion

I keep reporting and analysis distinct, and say when I am offering a judgement.

03

Corrections

If I find an error, I correct it and show the update date on the article.

04

Independence

This is a personal, independent project and carries no advertising.

Hasanur Rahman

I am a Mathematics Honours student with a strong interest in geopolitics, international relations, political science, history, strategic studies and global affairs.

My interests extend to Bangladesh’s politics, political history, foreign policy and governance, and the country’s position within regional and international geopolitical dynamics.

Through this site I am developing my analytical, research and academic writing skills, and building a space to explore and discuss important global and political issues.

My long-term aim is to pursue higher education and doctoral research, contribute to international affairs research, and work in policy analysis and strategic studies.
