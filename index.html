About

Beyond Headlines. Understanding the World.

This is an independent platform dedicated to exploring geopolitics, international relations, strategic affairs, political developments, history, and global issues.

The platform seeks to understand the world beyond headlines—examining the historical context, geopolitical interests, power dynamics, strategic calculations, and political forces that shape international events.

With a particular interest in Bangladesh, South Asia, and the broader international system, this platform aims to provide thoughtful analysis, informed perspectives, and a deeper understanding of complex global developments.

Mission

My mission is to make complex geopolitical and political developments more understandable through analytical writing, historical context, and critical examination.

I aim to:

  1. Analyze international affairs beyond surface-level news.
  2. Explore geopolitical competition, diplomacy, security, and strategic interests.
  3. Examine Bangladesh’s politics, history, governance, and foreign relations.
  4. Connect historical events with contemporary developments.
  5. Encourage critical thinking and informed public discourse.

Areas of Interest

Geopolitics & International Relations

Exploring global power competition, international alliances, diplomacy, foreign policy, and the evolving international order.

Strategic & Security Studies

Examining military strategy, national security, regional conflicts, deterrence, defense policy, and strategic competition.

Bangladesh & South Asian Affairs

Analyzing Bangladesh’s political landscape, history, governance, foreign relations, political economy, and regional dynamics.

History & Political Analysis

Understanding contemporary events through historical perspectives, political theories, institutions, ideologies, and power structures.

Global Affairs

Exploring major international developments, emerging geopolitical trends, economic relations, and the shifting balance of global power.

Analytical Approach

I believe that meaningful analysis requires more than simply following events. My approach emphasizes:

Historical Context
Understanding how the past shapes the present.
Geographical Perspective
Examining how geography, resources, borders, and strategic locations influence political decisions.
Multiple Perspectives
Considering competing interests, narratives, and interpretations.
Critical Thinking
Questioning assumptions, examining evidence, and distinguishing facts from opinions.
Analytical Depth
Exploring not only what is happening, but why it is happening and what it may mean.

About the Author

Hasanur Rahman

Hasanur Rahman is a Mathematics Honours student with a strong interest in geopolitics, international relations, political science, history, strategic studies, and global affairs.

His intellectual interests extend to Bangladesh’s politics, political history, foreign policy, governance, and the country’s position within regional and international geopolitical dynamics.

Through this platform, he aims to develop his analytical, research, and academic writing skills while building a platform for exploring and discussing important global and political issues.

His long-term aspiration is to pursue higher education and doctoral research, contribute to international affairs research, and engage with the broader world of policy analysis and strategic studies.

Vision

To build a credible analytical platform that contributes to a deeper understanding of geopolitics, international affairs, and political developments—and to grow into a meaningful space for research, analysis, and intellectual discussion.
